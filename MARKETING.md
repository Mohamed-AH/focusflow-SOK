# FocusFlow — Marketing Copy

Positioning, B2C and B2B messaging, and ready-to-post launch copy for Twitter/X
and LinkedIn.

---

## Positioning

**One-liner:** The habit tracker built for how you actually work.

**Elevator pitch:** FocusFlow is a fast, clean habit tracker that works instantly in
your browser — no signup required. Plan your day, check off activities, and watch
streaks compound. Sign in to sync across devices, and for coaches and teams, a
built-in admin dashboard handles member onboarding, localized metrics and
currencies, and engagement analytics.

**Brand voice:** Direct, confident, zero hype. We say what the product does and let
the streaks speak.

## B2C copy (individuals)

### Headline options

1. Track habits. Build streaks. Stay focused.
2. The habit tracker built for how you actually work.
3. Your day, planned and done — in one screen.
4. Consistency isn't a personality trait. It's a system.

### Body copy (website / app store)

> You don't need another productivity philosophy. You need to see, at a glance,
> whether today counted.
>
> FocusFlow gives you a daily plan you can check off in seconds, a progress ring
> that fills as you go, and a streak counter that makes consistency addictive.
> Separate profiles keep your student life, work life, side hustle, and family
> life from blurring together.
>
> It works the moment you open it — no account, no credit card, no onboarding
> quiz. Your data lives in your browser until you decide to sign in and sync it
> to the cloud. Then it follows you to every device.

### Feature bullets

- **One-screen days.** Plan activities, check them off, done. No nested projects, no config rabbit holes.
- **Streaks that motivate.** Hit your daily goal, extend the streak. Current run, best ever, perfect days.
- **Profiles for every role.** Student, professional, founder, creative, parent — separate routines, separate stats.
- **Analytics that teach you.** Time-of-day heatmaps, category breakdowns, week-over-week trends.
- **Private by default.** Works offline, in your browser, with zero data collected — until *you* opt into cloud sync.

### Email / lifecycle snippets

- Subject: *Your streak is waiting.* — "You've hit your goal 4 days in a row. Day 5 takes about 20 seconds to plan."
- Subject: *One screen. One day. Done.* — "FocusFlow now syncs across devices. Sign in once and your habits follow you everywhere."

## B2B copy (coaches, habit programs, teams)

### Headline options

1. Run habit programs your clients actually finish.
2. The habit-building platform for coaches who measure results.
3. Onboard clients in minutes. Watch consistency in real time.

### Body copy (sales page / one-pager)

> Habit coaches and wellness programs lose clients in the gap between sessions.
> FocusFlow closes it.
>
> Onboard each client from the admin dashboard with localized defaults — their
> language, their currency, their week structure. Clients sign in with Google or
> GitHub and get a habit tracker that works on any device, instantly.
>
> Then watch the data you've never had: daily active usage, average completion
> rates, streak leaderboards, and the activity categories your clients actually
> stick to. Spot the client who's about to churn before they do.

### Feature bullets

- **Client onboarding built in.** Pre-provision accounts by email; clients activate with one OAuth sign-in.
- **Localized for any market.** Set locale, currency, timezone, week start, and the tracking metrics you surface — per deployment.
- **Engagement analytics.** DAU trends, sync activity, completion averages, and streak leaderboards across your whole book of clients.
- **Account controls.** Deactivate, reactivate, or remove accounts — deactivated users are blocked at sign-in.
- **Runs on free infrastructure.** Render free tier + MongoDB Atlas M0. Your program's tooling costs $0 to pilot.
